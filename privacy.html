Privacy Policy

ECS Drive · Last updated: 21 September 2026

ECS Drive is a small, free website where college students share study files (notes, assignments, code) in named folders. This page explains what information the service handles and why. Back to ECS Drive

What we collect

  • Folders and files you add: folder names, file names, file contents, and the upload date and size.
  • Passwords and recovery answers: a folder's viewing password, the manager password and the two recovery answers are never stored as typed. Only irreversible scrambled values (hashes) are stored.
  • Technical data: your IP address and the kind of action taken (for example a failed password attempt or a folder creation). This is kept in a security log and is used only to limit abuse and investigate problems.
  • On your device: the site stores a sign-in session for folders you manage, and a short-lived unlock session for password-protected folders, in your browser's own storage. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

Google Drive and Google user data

Uploaded files are stored in a Google Drive account operated by the ECS Drive administrator (ecshub4u@gmail.com). The service connects to that one account with the administrator's own authorisation, using the Google Drive API, only to create folders and to store, list, download and delete the files that visitors upload through this site.

Visitors are never asked to sign in with Google, and this service does not access, read or collect any visitor's own Google account data.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How the information is used

  • To show folders and files, and to let managers rename or delete them.
  • To protect the service against abuse (rate limiting, security log).

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

The service runs on Cloudflare (application and database) and Google Drive (file storage). These providers process the data only to run the service.

Keeping and deleting data

Files stay until a folder's manager deletes them or the folder. Deleted files move to the Drive trash and are removed from there by the administrator. Security-log entries are kept for a limited time for abuse prevention. To ask for something to be removed, write to the address below.

Security

Connections use HTTPS. Access to management features needs the manager password. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not upload highly sensitive or confidential documents.

Contact

Questions or removal requests: ecshub4u@gmail.com

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
